# Brand logos

Web-ready marks for the “trusted brands / brands we service” row, starting from [Appliance Pro Today](https://www.applianceprotoday.com/) and filled in with every brand on our own list that they do not show.

Canonical files live in `svg/`, filled `#41495a` to match APT’s monochrome strip. Raster originals (when the official mark was not already a vector) are in `png/`. Inventory is `brands.json`. Open `preview.html` to see the strip.

These are third-party trademarks. The site disclaimer already states that listing them does not imply affiliation or endorsement.
